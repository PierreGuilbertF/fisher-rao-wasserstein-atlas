A Gaussian location variation and the associated distribution and quantile functions. For theta = (mu, sigma) = (0.40, 0.22) and dtheta = (0.10, 0), the left panel shows the zero-mass density variation u = d_theta p_theta(dtheta), shaded in red. The middle panel shows the cumulative distribution function F_theta and, separately, its parameter variation U(x) = integral from -infinity to x of u(s) ds = d_theta F_theta(dtheta)(x), drawn as a dashed red curve. The right panel shows Q_theta = F_theta^{-1}, with F_theta repeated faintly for comparison. Equal horizontal and vertical scales make their reflection across y = x visible; the marked points (x, r) and (r, x) satisfy F_theta(x) = r and Q_theta(r) = x. U is a variation of F_theta, not F_theta itself.
